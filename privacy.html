Adam QBank (Admin) - Privacy Policy

This administrator app stores your question banks, categories, settings and backups locally on your device, in a separate database for each specialty.

It contacts Firebase (Google) only when you use the Cloud features in Settings: it signs in with your admin account, uploads the question bank of the specialty you choose to publish, saves the specialty list, and reads student accounts and question reports so you can manage them.

No analytics services and no advertising networks are used.
